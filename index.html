What you're seeing

NASA's TOI-700 d catalog links directly to Eyes on Exoplanets, NASA/JPL's interactive 3D exoplanet environment. This project embeds that official explorer instead of pretending a custom-rendered sphere is the real planet.

Important scientific note

TOI-700 d has not been photographed as a resolved surface image. Any surface appearance shown by NASA or other visualizations is a model or artist's concept, not a direct photograph of the planet.

Habitable zone ≠ inhabited

TOI-700 d receives about 86% of Earth's incident stellar energy in the discovery analysis. Its orbit falls in the star's conservative habitable zone, but its actual atmosphere, surface water, and climate are not directly known.

Climate possibilities

Published 3D climate modeling examined 20 atmospheric/surface/rotation scenarios. Temperate conditions were possible in multiple modeled cases, while predicted spectral features were below 10 ppm.

05 / Reproducibility

Calculations & methodology

Relative bulk density

ρ/ρ⊕ = (M/M⊕)/(R/R⊕)³

Using 1.25 M⊕ and 1.073 R⊕ gives approximately 1.01 × Earth's mean density.

Kepler consistency check

M★ ≈ a³/P²

With a in AU and P in years, the simplified relation gives about 0.415 M☉. This is a consistency calculation, not a substitute for detailed stellar modeling.
